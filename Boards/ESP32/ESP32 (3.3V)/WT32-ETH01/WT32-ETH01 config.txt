With the factory firmware the WT32-ETH01 acts as a serial port to 
Ethernet module that listens to AT commands over the 3.3V serial port. 
But it is also a normal ESP32 which can be programmed with whatever 
sketch you like. The only drawback is that there is no USB port. 
Fortunately each ESP32 chip has a serial bootloader mode. To program it, 
please use a USB to TTL Adapter with levels set to 3.3V.

1. Connect it like:

WT32_ETH01                                            USB to TTL Adapter
----------                                            ------------------
GND                                  <->                             GND
GPIO3=RXD0 (MCU point of view)       <->        TX(D) (PC point of view)
GPIO1=TXD0 (MCU point of view)       <->        RX(D) (PC point of view)
5V or 3.3V                           <->                      5V or 3.3V

2. In Arduino IDE select:
   Tools - Board - esp32 - "WT32-ETH01 Ethernet Module"

3. Select USB to TTL Adapter's serial port and set baudrate to 115200.

4. Bring the WT32-EHT01 into bootloader mode:
   - As the board has a 10kΩ pull-up, connect GPIO0 with a strong 
     pull-down (4.7kΩ or less) to GND. In addition, GPIO2 must be either 
     left unconnected, or driven LOW.
   - Reset by shortly connecting EN to GND, you should now see the 
     "waiting for download" message.

5. Choose your sketch and click on the Arduino IDE's Upload button.

6. When the Upload finishes, disconnect GPIO0 and reset.
