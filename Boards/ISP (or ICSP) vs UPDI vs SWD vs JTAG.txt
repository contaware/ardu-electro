Overview
--------

- ISP (In System Programming) and ICSP (In Circuit Serial Programming) 
  are the same protocol/interface that uses SPI and the reset pin to 
  program ATmega328P, ATmega2560, ATmega32U4 and ATtiny85 chips.

- UPDI (Unified Program and Debug Interface) is a 1-wire protocol used 
  for ATmega4809 and newer ATtinys.

- SWD (Serial Wire Debug) is an ARM specific protocol/interface designed 
  for debugging and flashing used for RA4M1, SAMD21, SAMD51, SAM3X8E 
  and also STM32.

- JTAG (Joint Test Action Group) was designed largely for chip and board 
  testing. Debugging and flashing micros was an evolution in its 
  application over time. The ESP32 and ESP8266 use JTAG.
